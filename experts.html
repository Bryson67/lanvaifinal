Lanvai Marketing

Protection · Structure · Right Partnerships

Influencers across Kenya have recently faced a tough reality — being brought on board to promote brands, only for the company’s reputation to later collapse, leaving the influencer unfairly labeled as a fraud. This is not just frustrating — it’s damaging to your personal brand and long-term credibility.

At Lanvai Marketing, we believe influencers deserve protection, structure, and the right partnerships.

We work with influencers to:

  • ✔️ Vet brands before any collaboration
  • ✔️ Structure clear, legally sound contracts
  • ✔️ Protect your reputation and long-term value
  • ✔️ Ensure transparency in every deal

For companies and brands:
We connect you with the right influencers — not just popular names, but individuals who truly align with your brand values, audience, and goals. Not every influencer is the right fit for every campaign, and that’s where we come in.

We handle:

  • ✔️ Influencer sourcing and vetting
  • ✔️ Campaign alignment and strategy
  • ✔️ Contract negotiation and management
  • ✔️ Long-term partnership building

Let’s build smarter, safer, and more impactful collaborations.
