Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enroll in a course?
Browse our courses on the Students page, click "Enroll Now" on your chosen course, and fill out the enrollment form. You'll receive a confirmation within 24 hours.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept Bank Transfer, M-Pesa, Credit/Debit Cards, and PayPal. All payments are securely processed.
Do you offer certificates?
Yes! Upon successful completion of any course, you receive a verifiable certificate from the institution and Lanvai Courses.
How long does enrollment take?
Enrollment processing typically takes 24-48 hours. You'll receive a welcome email with course access details.
Can I get a refund?
Yes, we offer a 7-day money-back guarantee for all courses. Contact us within 7 days for a full refund.
Do you have study abroad options?
Yes! We partner with universities in the UK, USA, Canada, Australia, Germany, and more. Contact us for details.
